Powered by TrainingPeaks

Your training, live in your pocket.

Every session lives in the TrainingPeaks app with exact targets. Train, sync, and I see it. Here's how a week works.

  • 1
    Your week lands in TrainingPeaks

    Structured workouts with clear targets for pace, power, heart rate and swim intervals, scheduled on your calendar.

  • 2
    Workouts sync to your watch

    Sessions push straight to your Garmin, COROS, Wahoo, Suunto or Apple Watch, or to Zwift for indoor rides. Just press start.

  • 3
    Completed data uploads automatically

    Finish the session and it syncs back. Each workout is colour-coded green, yellow or red to show how closely it matched the plan.

  • 4
    I review it and reply

    Comments on your workouts, plus a WhatsApp if something needs a chat. The plan is adjusted live, not at the end of the month.

  • 5
    Track your fitness over time

    Training load, fitness and fatigue trends show you're building toward race day, and help time your taper perfectly.

About Bridget

I'm a professional triathlete from South Africa, racing the IRONMAN Pro Series.

I started in short-course racing, competing for South Africa on the World Triathlon circuit and on Africa Triathlon Cup podiums, before moving up to the 70.3 distance.

In 2026 I raced the IRONMAN 70.3 World Championship in Nice, finished 4th at IRONMAN 70.3 Krakow and won the South African Cross Triathlon Championship.

I know what it takes to balance hard training with real life, and I coach the way I'd want to be coached: personal, honest and always reachable.
